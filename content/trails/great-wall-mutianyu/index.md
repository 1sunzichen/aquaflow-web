## Why Mutianyu

Mutianyu is the sweet spot for hikers visiting the Great Wall. It's less crowded than Badaling, better restored than Jiankou, and the surrounding forest scenery is stunning year-round. Most importantly, the trail is long enough to feel like a real adventure without requiring technical climbing skills.

## Getting There

**By public bus (budget option)**

From Dongzhimen Transport Hub (东直门交通枢纽), take Bus 867 directly to Mutianyu. The ride takes about 1.5 hours and costs ¥12. Buses depart from 6:30 AM, with the last return around 5:00 PM.

**By chartered van (group option)**

Shared charter vans from Dongzhimen area run ¥300–500 for the whole vehicle. Great if you're a group of 4–6 people. Negotiate a return pickup time when you hire the driver.

## Entrance & Tickets

- **Entrance fee**: ¥65 per person
- **Cable car up**: ¥100 (highly recommended if you want to save legs for the wall itself)
- **Toboggan slide down**: ¥100 (fun, but expect queues on weekends)
- **Opening hours**: 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM (gates close at 5:00 PM)

Buy tickets at the main gate. No need to book in advance on weekdays.

## The Hike

Once you're on the wall, the main walkable section runs from Tower 6 to Tower 23 — about 4 km round trip. The path is mostly stone steps, with some steeper sections between towers 14–20.

**Recommended route:**

1. Take cable car to Tower 14
2. Walk east to Tower 23 (45 min, downhill, great views)
3. Return west to Tower 6 (1 hr, more strenuous, fewer crowds)
4. Take the toboggan slide or cable car back down

**Tips:**

- Towers 6 and 23 are the endpoints — beyond them the wall becomes unrestored and access is restricted
- Tower 20 has the best panoramic view; plan your photos here
- Bring your own water. Vendors on the wall charge ¥10–20 per bottle

## What to Bring

- Comfortable hiking shoes with grip (stone steps get slippery in rain)
- 1.5L water minimum
- Sun protection (very exposed on the wall)
- Light jacket — the ridge is windy even in summer
- Small snacks (no real food options on the wall)

## Best Time to Visit

| Season | Condition |
|--------|-----------|
| April–May | Cherry blossoms below, clear skies |
| June–August | Lush green, but hot and sometimes hazy |
| September–October | Best overall: cool, golden foliage |
| November–March | Quiet and dramatic, dress warmly |

Avoid national holidays (Golden Week in early May and October) — the wall gets extremely crowded.

## Nearby

After the hike, the village below has several family-run restaurants serving local dishes. The **trout** from local streams is a regional specialty worth trying.

If you have time, the **Hongluo Temple** (红螺寺) is 20 minutes away by taxi and makes for a peaceful afternoon addition.
